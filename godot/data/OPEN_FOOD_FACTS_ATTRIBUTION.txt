Data attribution for the bundled barcode_catalog.json

The offline product names, barcodes and nutrition facts were retrieved from
Open Food Facts (https://world.openfoodfacts.org) on 2026-11-19.

Open Food Facts data is available under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0.
License: https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/
Original service: https://world.openfoodfacts.org
Project data and source: https://github.com/Domenklay/My_balance

The embedded subset may contain incomplete or incorrect contributor-entered
information. Please always check the product label and portion units before
using a food entry. Products can change their recipes without changing barcode.

User-entered food diary entries are stored separately and are not uploaded
to Open Food Facts.
